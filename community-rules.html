Community Rules

Last updated: February 2026 — contact support@rulebritannia.org

Rule Britannia is a political simulation. These rules ensure the community remains safe, fair, and enjoyable for all participants. All users must follow them at all times.

1. Respect

  • Treat all users with respect. Personal attacks, harassment, and targeted abuse are prohibited.
  • Disagreement is expected in a political simulation — keep it in character and within the rules.
  • Discrimination on the basis of race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, age, or any other protected characteristic is strictly prohibited.

2. Honest play

  • No multi-accounting. Each person may have one active account.
  • Do not impersonate real politicians, public figures, or other users.
  • Do not share account credentials or act on behalf of another account.
  • Do not collude to manipulate simulation outcomes in ways that violate the spirit of the rules.

3. In-character conduct

  • Keep out-of-character (OOC) conduct clearly separated from in-character (IC) posts.
  • Political content within the simulation is fictional and should not be mistaken for real-world advocacy.
  • Extreme or deliberately offensive in-character positions that serve no narrative purpose are prohibited.

4. Content standards

  • No graphic violence, sexual content, or content harmful to minors.
  • No spam, advertising, or off-topic promotional content.
  • No content that is illegal under UK law.

5. Platform use

  • Do not attempt to disrupt, exploit, or abuse the platform infrastructure.
  • Do not use automated tools or scripts to interact with the simulation without permission.

6. Enforcement

Violations may result in warnings, content removal, temporary suspension, or permanent ban, at the discretion of the moderation team. Severe or repeat violations will be handled more seriously. All moderation decisions are final unless appealed to support@rulebritannia.org.

7. Reporting

To report a violation, see our Moderation & Reporting page.
