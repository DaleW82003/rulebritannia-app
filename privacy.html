Privacy Notice

Last updated: February 2026 — contact support@rulebritannia.org

1. Who we are

Rule Britannia (“we”, “us”, “our”) is a UK-oriented political simulation platform operated at rulebritannia.org. For queries about this notice, contact us at support@rulebritannia.org.

2. What data we collect

  • Email address — required to create an account and to send transactional emails (e.g. email verification).
  • Username and display name — chosen by you during registration; visible to other users within the simulation.
  • Password — stored as a one-way bcrypt hash. We never store your plaintext password.
  • Age attestation — a simple confirmation that you are 16 or older. No date of birth is collected or stored.
  • IP address and rate-limiting data — used for abuse prevention and security. Stored transiently in memory and/or server logs.
  • Audit logs — records of significant administrative actions (e.g. approvals, role changes) are kept for moderation and transparency. These reference user IDs, not full personal details.
  • Marketing consent — if you opt in to marketing emails during registration, we record your consent and the timestamp of that consent.

We do not collect date of birth, phone numbers, real-world addresses, payment details, or special-category personal data.

3. How we use your data

  • To create and manage your account and application.
  • To send you a verification email when you register (transactional; you cannot opt out of this).
  • To send you optional news and updates, only if you have opted in during registration.
  • To prevent abuse and enforce our Community Rules and Terms of Use.
  • To integrate with our community forum (Discourse) where SSO is enabled — see Section 6.

4. Anti-bot protection (Cloudflare Turnstile)

Our registration form may use Cloudflare Turnstile, a privacy-preserving anti-bot challenge. When Turnstile is active, a small script from Cloudflare runs in your browser to verify you are human. Cloudflare processes data subject to their own Privacy Policy. No personal data is shared with Cloudflare beyond what the challenge requires.

5. Legal basis (UK GDPR)

We rely on the following legal bases:

  • Contract performance — processing necessary to provide the service you applied for.
  • Legitimate interests — security, fraud prevention, and audit logging.
  • Consent — marketing emails (opt-in only; you may withdraw consent at any time by emailing support@rulebritannia.org).

6. Discourse forum integration (SSO)

If you access the Rule Britannia community forum (forum subdomain), your username, display name, email address, and assigned roles are shared with the Discourse forum software via DiscourseConnect SSO. This enables single sign-on and role-based group membership on the forum. No additional personal data is shared beyond what is listed above.

7. Data retention

  • Active user accounts are retained for as long as the service operates or until you request deletion.
  • Rejected or withdrawn applications are retained for a limited period for abuse-prevention purposes, then deleted.
  • Audit log entries are retained for the life of the service.

8. Your rights (UK GDPR)

You have the right to access, correct, or erase your personal data, to restrict or object to processing, and to data portability. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@rulebritannia.org. We will respond within one month.

9. Cookies and sessions

We use a single session cookie (rb.sid) to maintain your login state. This cookie is strictly necessary and does not track you across third-party sites. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies.

10. Contact us

For any data protection queries: support@rulebritannia.org.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO).
