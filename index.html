Rule Britannia

A live, database-backed political simulation platform where players govern, legislate, debate, and compete within a structured parliamentary system driven by automation and enforced process.

Apply to Join Sign In

What it Does

  • Runs a persistent UK political simulation with a live backend state.
  • Enforces real parliamentary processes (stages, amendments, divisions, deadlines).
  • Automates voting, weighted majorities, and delegation for absences.
  • Tracks government and opposition roles dynamically.
  • Simulates Question Time with deadlines and structured follow-ups.
  • Integrates press, polling, and economic indicators into the political environment.
  • Logs all administrative actions for transparency and moderation stability.
  • Separates demo mode (public preview) from live simulation state.

The Simulation is Live

Every vote, every debate, every division — happening now.

How it Works

From application to office in five steps.

  1. Create Your Character Apply to join, get approved by an admin, and set up your player profile.
  2. Take Office & Act Receive a parliamentary role, join a party, and begin participating in government or opposition.
  3. Debate & Vote Engage in bills, questions, motions, and debates; cast divisions; represent your constituents.
  4. Simulation Advances The clock ticks forward automatically, advancing parliamentary business, elections, and the economy.
  5. Live State Persists Every action is recorded. The simulation state is always live, accessible, and historically logged.
