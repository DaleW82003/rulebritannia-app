Roles in the simulation

Player (MP)

Core participants representing seats and advancing their political agenda.

  • Submit bills, questions, motions, and statements
  • Vote in divisions and join debates
  • Balance party, media, and constituency commitments

Party Leadership

Sets strategic direction and messaging for each parliamentary party.

  • Assign frontbench roles and priorities
  • Coordinate legislative and media strategy
  • Negotiate coalition or confidence arrangements

Chief Whip

Manages vote discipline so party plans survive key parliamentary moments.

  • Issue voting guidance and attendance expectations
  • Track support before divisions close
  • Escalate discipline issues through party channels

Speaker / Mods

Keeps process fair and resolves edge cases under published rules.

  • Adjudicate disputes and procedural ambiguity
  • Confirm outcomes and timeline consistency
  • Maintain continuity of the shared world state

Admin

Operates the platform, onboarding, and policy framework for the sim.

  • Approve applications and assign access
  • Configure simulation settings and tooling
  • Oversee moderation standards and platform health

FAQ

Is this live 24/7 or session-based?

The world state is persistent 24/7, with key activity peaks around scheduled session nights and voting windows.

Do I need deep Westminster knowledge?

No. The system is structured so motivated newcomers can learn by doing, while experienced players still have strategic depth.

What is public versus logged-in?

This landing page is public. Active gameplay tools, records, and role actions are available once your account is approved.

How are disputes and scandals handled?

Moderators apply published rules, adjudicate edge cases, and then update the official timeline with the final ruling.

Can I play casually?

Yes. Casual play is viable at around 15–30 minutes daily, with optional higher involvement during major debates or divisions.

When does Discourse integration happen?

Debate links are designed to map bills and motions to discussion threads so argument history can feed directly into outcomes.

Do moderators control political outcomes?

Routine outcomes come from player actions and voting rules; moderators mainly intervene for disputes, edge cases, and consistency.

Can I switch roles later?

Yes, role assignments can evolve through in-sim political changes or admin decisions, depending on party and parliamentary dynamics.
