Moderation & Reporting

Last updated: February 2026

Rule Britannia is moderated by a volunteer team. This page explains how to report issues and what to expect from the moderation process.

How to report

If you witness a rule violation, harassment, or any other concern, please use one of the following channels:

  • Email: support@rulebritannia.org — for account issues, ban appeals, or sensitive reports.
  • Discord: discord.gg/rbarAWGy — post in the moderation channel or DM a moderator. Best for quick queries.

What to include in a report

  • Your username and the username of the person you are reporting (if applicable).
  • A clear description of what happened.
  • Any relevant screenshots, links, or evidence.
  • The approximate date and time of the incident.

What happens after a report

  1. We aim to acknowledge reports within 48 hours.
  2. The moderation team will review the report and any evidence.
  3. Appropriate action will be taken in accordance with our Community Rules and Terms of Use.
  4. We will update you on the outcome where appropriate, though we may not be able to share details of sanctions applied to other users.

Ban appeals

If your account has been suspended or banned and you believe this was in error, email support@rulebritannia.org with your username and a clear explanation. Appeals are reviewed on a case-by-case basis.

Urgent safety issues

If you are concerned about someone’s immediate safety, please contact the relevant emergency services first. For platform-level threats or serious legal concerns, email support@rulebritannia.org and we will respond as quickly as possible.
